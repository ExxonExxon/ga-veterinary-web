# 03 — On-Page SEO

### Summary
This is the weakest area. Meta descriptions are 85% default boilerplate, the homepage H1 is empty, the homepage hero renders as a blank black box (so the primary head element and LCP are broken), several titles are keyword-stuffed or truncated with a dangling `-`, and a two-page duplicate-title collision exists on the single most important commercial query. Content depth is thin across the board.

### Issues Found

| Issue | Severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low) | Affected URLs (sample) | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta descriptions are default WordPress boilerplate on 28 of 33 pages** — literally `Just another WordPress site` (27 chars). The 5 non-default ones are still far too short (`Retractor` = 9 chars, `Custom Surgical Instruments` = 27 chars). | **Critical** | `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/faq/`, `/needles/`, `/kocher-forceps/`, `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/`, `/our-capabilities/`, all `client-item`, `offer-item`, `portfolio-item`, `portfolio-types`, `vgag`, `/site-map/` | Write unique, compelling 140–155 char meta descriptions on every indexable page. This is the single highest-paid-off on-page fix. |
| **Empty H1 on the homepage** — the rendered DOM contains exactly one `<h1></h1>` with no text. | **Critical** | `/` | Put a real, keyword-relevant H1 on the homepage (e.g. "Custom Surgical & Veterinary Instruments – Designed & Made in Australia"). |
| **Homepage hero is a blank black box** — Slider Revolution slides do not render; the hero area shows only carousel arrows. Confirmed in raw HTML (`rev_slider_wrapper` with `background:#000000`) and desktop + mobile screenshots. | **Critical** | `/` | Replace Slider Revolution with a static, crawlable hero (image + H1 + text). This also fixes the 2.4 s LCP and the "above-the-fold is invisible" problem. |
| **Duplicate title tags**: `/about/` and `/custom-instruments/` both use `GA Medical | Custom Surgical Instruments Prototyping & Development`. | High | `/about/`, `/custom-instruments/` | Give each a distinct title; they must not cannibalize the "custom surgical instruments" query. |
| **Keyword-stuffed title**: `/craniotomyhook/` title is `Craniotomy Hooks - Scalp Retractor - Fish Hook Retractor - Scalp Hook` (4 hyphens, stuffing 4 synonyms). | High | `/craniotomyhook/` | Rewrite to a natural 50–60 char title, e.g. "Titanium Craniotomy Scalp Hooks" (matches H1 and content). |
| **ALL-CAPS spammy title**: `/bone-lever/` → `BONE LEVER Trethowan SUPERIOR STIFFNESS & Resistant to Bending`. | High | `/bone-lever/` | Lowercase and rewrite naturally: "Lane Trethowan Bone Lever – Superior Stiffness". |
| **Truncated titles with a dangling ` -`** across ~14 pages (a Yoast/title-template bug where the site-name separator is left empty): `Kocher Forceps -`, `gag -`, `Gag Dental -`, `Mammal Gag -`, `Themeforest -`, `3docean -`, `GA Medical - Soft Tissue Retractor -`. | Medium | `/kocher-forceps/`, `/vgag/*`, `/client-item/*`, `/retractor/` | Fix the title template so no trailing separator; write real titles for these pages. |
| **Title length**: several are far too short (`Kocher Forceps -` 16 chars, `gag -` 5 chars) or slightly long (69-71 chars on `/needles/`, `/craniotomyhook/`, `/contact/`, `/faq/`). | Medium | as above | Target 50–60 characters; stop at the leading query, don't pad. |
| **H1 > title duplication**: on `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` the H1 is just `Veterinary` (title is the good, specific `GA Medical Custom Made Veterinary Instruments - Avian, Wildlife Surgery`). H1 should not be a one-word fragment. | High | `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` | Set H1 to the specific product line ("Custom Made Veterinary Instruments for Avian & Wildlife Surgery"). |
| **Heading hierarchy skip / missing H2 context**: several templates have H2/H3 counts that don't build a logical outline — e.g. `/about/` has exactly one H1 and zero H2/H3; `/contact/` has one H1 and three H3 but no H2; `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` has one H1 and nothing else. | Medium | `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/`, `/needles/`, `/kocher-forceps/` | Add H2 section headings that reflect real content sections, in order. |
| **Thin content** (see 04): 24 pages under 200 words, many in the 83–155 range. Keyword-rich titles on `/needles/` (155w), `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` (95w), `/kocher-forceps/` (83w) are not backed by copy. | High | see 04-content-eeat.md | Write 400–800+ words per money page. |
| **URL structure** is clean and readable (lowercase, hyphenated, logical): `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/`, `/portfolio-item/design-engineering/`. No ugly parameters. | Good | all | Keep. Consider shortening `craniotomyhook` → `/craniotomy-hooks/` for readability, and resolve the `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` vs `/custom-instruments/` semantically if both stay. |
| Keyword in URL is present on product pages but **keyword in first 100 words is inconsistent** — several product pages lead with generic copy; ensure primary keyword appears near the top. | Medium | `/needles/`, `/kocher-forceps/`, `/custom-made-veterinary-instruments/` | Rewrite opening paragraphs to state the primary keyword + unique value in the first 100 words. |
