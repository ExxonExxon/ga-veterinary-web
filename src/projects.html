GA Medical Veterinary

Projects

Surgical instruments, orthopaedic devices, consumables and apparatus for wildlife, marine mammals, reptiles, fish, and birds.

Saving wildlife

I develop custom veterinary medical devices for animals that most companies won't touch, because it's not "cost effective" for them. Most of my projects are for a single animal or a small group.

Veterinary engineering

Click any image to enlarge

Micro Skeletal Fixation Pins

For broken bones in the smallest birds and animals. The pins are antibacterial and unusually strong.

Custom Made Pelvic Implant

TPO pelvic implant designed to expand the pelvic cavity of a Sun Bear to reduce daily pain. Read more at orangutanfoundation.org.au

Fragment Grasping Kocher Forceps

Re-engineered forceps for manipulating small fragments and implants without deforming the tool.

Micro Laryngoscope Blades

Developed for small bird and animal endotracheal tubing and oral examination.

Micro Orthopaedic Pin Chuck

Pin chuck for controlled insertion of micro pins during orthopaedic procedures on small animals.

Mini Orthopaedic Hand Chuck

Hand-operated chuck providing fine manual control over micro pins and wires during placement.

Table Mounted Veterinary Gag

Unique gag with cheek dilators for oral procedures on tight-gaped Australian mammals.

Micro External Skeletal Fixation Joints

Extra small, lightweight joints for bird wing fractures to reduce strain and improve healing.

Extra Long Hypodermic Needles

Designed for the humane euthanasia of beached whales that cannot be returned to sea.

High Friction ESF Connecting Rods

Developed to minimize bone malunion by reducing fixation device slippage.

Extra Large Needle & PGA Suture Kits

Extra strong kits for Grey Nurse Shark surgery, featuring large needles and dissolvable sutures.

Custom Made Laryngoscope

Built for tight-gaped Australian mammals.

Titanium Surgical Hook Retractors

Hand-made, rust-proof retractors for marine animal surgery in high-salt environments.

Dolphin Dorsal Fin Corer

For biopsies and GPS attachment; includes a drilling guide and sharpening file.

Avian Leg Bands

Custom identification bands for avian patients, designed for tracking and monitoring wild bird populations.

K-Wire Kit & Wire Gauge

Kirschner wire kit for orthopaedic fixation in small animals, including a wire gauge for precise measurement and size selection.

The design, engineering and development is mostly self-funded, so there's a limit to what I can take on. The process is slow and expensive, and I wish I could do more. You can help too. With more support, I could get veterinarians the devices they need to make their animals' lives better.
